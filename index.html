I'm a senior undergraduate at Zhejiang University, pursuing a double degree in Computer Science and Finance. I'm currently a research intern at UMass Amherst, supervised by Prof. Chuang Gan, working on action-conditioned world models and humanoid robots. DéjàWorld: Scaling Action-Conditioned World Models through In-Context Dynamics Grounding
Zheng Cao*, Yuncong Yang*, Jiaben Chen, Kushaan Naskar, Haoyu Zhen, Chuang Gan
Under review, 2026
project page

We enable action-conditioned world models to scale at both training and test time, and to generalize across robotics setups that differ in embodiment, camera view, and action convention.

HuScale: Scalable Task-Level Data Generation for Humanoid Loco-Manipulation with KeypointVLA
Guanyu Cai*, Zheng Cao*, Quanbao Lin, Tengjie Zhu, Guanzhu Ren, Yu Zhang, Tianchen Deng, Yao Mu
Under review, 2026

We compose reusable atomic skills into task-level humanoid loco-manipulation demonstrations without human motion data, and use them to train KeypointVLA, which transfers zero-shot to a real Unitree G1.

Mitigating Conversational Inertia in Multi-Turn Agents
Yang Wan, Zheng Cao, Zhenhao Zhang, Zhengwen Zeng, Shuheng Shen, Changhua Meng, Linchao Zhu
ICML, 2026
project page / arXiv

We identify conversational inertia, where LLM agents imitate their own previous responses, and mitigate it with Context Preference Learning and inference-time context management.
